Page 2 · The client side

How it works for your clients

Six steps. The habit stays the same as texting you a photo, but now it's tracked automatically. Clients use it at fuel.theflexfacility.com.

01

Quick intake chat

2–3 minutes on first login. The AI asks about their body, goal, activity, food restrictions, and any health flags.

02

Daily targets set

The AI sets a calorie and protein target. You approve or edit it with one tap before it goes live.

03

Snap the meal

Photo or typed. The AI estimates calories and macros, and the client confirms or fixes it.

"About 550 cal, 45g protein. Log it?"
04

Nudges + affirmations

Reminders around meal times, plus motivation in their style: hype, calm, faith-based, or tough love.

"Haven't seen lunch yet. Snap it before you eat."
05

Ask anything

Log a weigh-in, or ask "How am I doing today?" or "What should I eat for dinner to stay on track?"

06

Monthly progress report

On the 1st, every client gets a progress summary by email and inside the app.

Built-in safety guardrails

Calorie floors. Targets never go below about 1,200 for women and 1,500 for men. You can adjust these.

Weight-loss cap. The AI limits how fast it targets weight loss.

Health check at intake. Pregnancy, diabetes, conditions, and medications get flagged to you before you approve.

Not medical advice. Photo counts are estimates. Flex Fuel is a coaching tool, not a dietitian.

Coach tip: If a client skips step 3, reminders still reach them by email. But home-screen setup gets the best results, so do it together. While you're there, have them join the step challenge too (page 5).

Log every day

Upload proof of the day's total steps. The Fuel Agent reads the number, checks the photo and moves them up the live Top 10 board.

Counts
  • A photo of the stair machine with the display switched to total steps
  • A screenshot of the day's Steps in a watch or phone health app (Apple Health/Fitness, Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung and others)
  • The full screen, no crops, filters or edits
Doesn't count
  • Steps per minute (the StairMaster's default display)
  • A workout summary with only time, calories, distance or pace
  • Edited, reused or AI-made images
  • Logging one day from two sources. Only the best entry per day counts

Each day can be logged until 36 hours after it ends. The leaderboard shows first name and last initial only. Photos, emails and phone numbers are never public.

Cost, rollout and decisions

PROPOSED COST
~$50
per month, all clients

Covers the AI cost, about $0.50–$1 per active client, or roughly $30–$60 for 60 clients.

Three ways you can use it

  • AFree perk for every client, to keep people around longer.
  • BBuilt into packages as part of your training price.
  • CPaid add-on sold as premium nutrition coaching.

Rollout plan

1Pick the pilot8–10 of your most consistent clients.
2Onboard in personWalk each one through page 4 at the gym.
3Review togetherCheck logging, accuracy and feedback. Fix what's off.
4Full rosterRoll out to all 60 clients.

The step challenge doesn't wait on the pilot. It's open to everyone from Nov 1, and it's an easy way to get the whole roster using the Fuel Agent.
