November Stair-Step Challenge

Nov 1 – Nov 24, 2026 · Central Time

Log your steps every day from Nov 1 to Nov 24. Finish with more total steps than Coach Kenny and you earn a free pass to his Thanksgiving Boot Camp.

  1. Log each day with a photo of the stair machine display after your workout, or a screenshot from your watch or phone health app (Apple Fitness/Health, Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung and others).
  2. One number counts per day: your best approved entry for that date. Logging the same day from two sources does not stack, because your watch already counts your stair steps.
  3. You can log a day until 36 hours after it ends. The last chance for every day is Wed Nov 25 at 12:00 PM Central.
  4. Show the full screen. No crops, no filters, no edits.
  5. Every photo is checked. Edited, reused or AI-made images do not count, and repeat offenders are removed from the challenge.
  6. Entries over 60,000 steps in a day, or far above your normal, are checked by hand before they count.
  7. Anyone in the Top 10 or ahead of Coach Kenny has every entry re-confirmed by Coach Kenny before a reward is issued.
  8. You must finish with MORE total steps than Coach Kenny (a tie does not win). Coach Kenny's total is public all month.
  9. The leaderboard shows first name and last initial only. Photos, emails and phone numbers are never public.

Fair play

The Fuel Agent checks every photo. Edited, reused or AI-made images don't count, and repeat offenders are removed. Coach Kenny has the final say on every entry.
