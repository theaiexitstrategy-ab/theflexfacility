    FAQ

    Questions

    Who can join?

    Anyone training with Coach Kenny, or anyone up for the challenge. It's free.

    What counts as proof?

    Anything that shows your total steps. On the stair machine, switch the display to total steps before you step off and take a photo (steps per minute doesn't count). From a watch or phone, screenshot today's Steps in your health app (Apple Health/Fitness, Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung and others). A workout summary with only time, distance or pace won't work. Show the full screen, with no crops or filters.

    Can I log the same day twice?

    Yes, but only your best entry for each day counts. A stair machine photo and a watch screenshot for the same day don't add together, because your watch already counts your stair steps.

    I forgot to log yesterday. Can I still?

    Yes. Each day stays open for 36 hours after it ends. The last chance for every day is Wednesday, Nov 25 at 12:00 PM Central.

    Why does my entry say "being checked"?

    Some photos get a second look from Coach Kenny's team before they count. You'll see the result on your My Challenge page.

    How do I win?

    Finish Nov 24 with more total steps than Coach Kenny. A tie doesn't win. Everyone who beats him earns a free pass to his Thanksgiving Boot Camp. Final counts are confirmed by Kenny before passes go out.

    Is my info public?

    No. The leaderboard shows your first name and last initial only. Photos, last names, emails and phone numbers are never shown.
